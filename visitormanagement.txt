1. Visitor Entry & Experience
Touchless QR Code Check-In/Out: Enables visitors to scan a code using their own smartphones for a contactless experience.

Self-Service Kiosk: A tablet-based interface (iPad/Android) for automated visitor registration.

Pre-Registration: Allows hosts to send digital invites with QR codes to scheduled visitors for faster entry.

Returning Visitor Recognition: Auto-populates data for frequent visitors to reduce check-in time.

Bulk Invites: Supports uploading large groups of visitors via CSV for event or group management.

Digital Signatures for NDAs: Captures digital signatures on legal documents and Non-Disclosure Agreements during check-in.

2. Security & Verification
ID Scanning & Photo Capture: Uses the device camera to scan government IDs and take visitor photos for verification.

OTP Verification: Sends a One-Time Password to the visitor's mobile number for identity authentication.

Host Approval Workflow: Provides an "Accept/Reject" feature where hosts must approve a visitor's entry via their mobile app.

Watchlist/Blocklist: Flags restricted individuals by name, email, or company to prevent unauthorized access.

Overstay Alerts: Notifies security if a visitor remains on the premises past their scheduled duration.

Health Questionnaires: Digital forms for health declarations and COVID-19 protocol screening.

3. Communication & Notifications
Instant Host Notifications: Alerts employees of visitor arrivals via SMS, email, or push notifications.

Customizable Email/SMS Alerts: Admins can configure automated messaging based on specific triggers.

Two-Way Communication: Allows hosts to communicate with the front desk or visitors before they enter the lobby.

4. Administration & Compliance
Centralized Web Dashboard: A unified interface to manage multiple locations, floors, and kiosks.

Digital Visitor Logs: Real-time searchable records of all visitor movements, replacing manual paper registers.

Analytics & Reporting: Provides graphical insights into visitor trends, peak times, and occupancy.

Multi-Location Management: Standardizes visitor workflows and data access across various office branches.

Data Security & Privacy: Features encrypted data storage and role-based access controls to meet GDPR and HIPAA standards.

5. Customization & Integration
White-Labeling/Branding: Allows companies to customize the kiosk UI with their own logos, colors, and themes.

Custom Visitor Types: Create unique workflows for different categories like contractors, vendors, or interviewees.

Third-Party Integrations: Connects with workplace tools like Slack, Google Workspace (Directory/Calendar), and Active Directory.
